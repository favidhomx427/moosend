## What is Moosend? Is it worth it?

[Moosend](https://moosend.com/) is an email marketing and automation platform built for small-to-mid-sized businesses, ecommerce stores, agencies, and lean marketing teams. Founded in 2011 and acquired by [Sitecore](https://www.sitecore.com/) in 2021, it has carved out a reputation as one of the **most affordable full-featured email tools on the market**, at roughly 4–6x cheaper than Klaviyo, Mailchimp, ActiveCampaign, and Omnisend at the same contact tier.

### What Moosend Does Well

- **Unlimited email sends on every plan**, most competitors meter you by email volume; Moosend meters by contact count only.
- **AI Writer with 7 tone options**, Subject Line Optimizer with benchmark scoring, and per-subscriber send-time optimization, all on the Pro plan.
- **CSA-certified deliverability**, Moosend scores 92/100 on independent deliverability testing.
- **Drag-and-drop automation builder** with conditional logic, web tracking, and segmentation.
- **30-day free trial with full Pro access**, no credit card required.

### Where Moosend Falls Short

- **No multi-channel marketing**, no native SMS, WhatsApp, push notifications or live chat widget. If you need email + SMS in one workflow, Brevo or Omnisend is the better choice.
- **No native Shopify integration** as of 2026, ecommerce setups require Zapier or custom API work.
- **Moosend+ and Enterprise pricing is not public**, you'll need to contact sales for a quote on dedicated IPs, transactional emails, or SSO.
- **No money-back guarantee**, the 30-day free trial is the only risk-free way to test.

### Who Should Use Moosend?

Moosend is an excellent fit if you:

- Run a small business, newsletter, blog, or agency client account
- Need solid automation and segmentation at a budget price
- Send high email volumes (Moosend's unlimited send model rewards you here)
- Want a clean, fast platform without a learning curve

It's a poor fit if you need multi-channel campaigns, deep ecommerce integration with Shopify, or built-in CRM/sales pipeline tooling.
